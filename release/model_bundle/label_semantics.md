# Label Semantics and Clinical Governance Specification

- **Target Label**: Centroids of normal-appearing retinal ganglion cell (RGC) axons.
- **Unit of Measurement**: `normal_appearing_axons`
- **Exclusion Boundaries**:
  - The absence of a point annotation does not prove the absence of an axon; degenerated axons, collapsed myelin sheaths, glia, and blood vessels are unannotated by design.
  - Never describe outputs as "total axon count" or "viable RGC count".
